# 일상 보스전 (Everyday Boss Fights)
Design read: neal.fun-style browser toys for anyone who has lost to a household chore; warm, silly, hand-made.
Concept spine: each chore is a "boss fight" nobody ever lets you practice. The hub is a roster of bosses.
Delivery tier: editorial (toy-first, micro-motion only)
Animation mode: non-animated — user picked Non-animated at intake
Locked palette: cool paper #F3F5FA, ink #1B1F2A, sheet blue #7EB6F2, tomato #FF5A4E, butter #FFE9A8, mint #CDEFD9, lilac #E3E4F5. Defense: bedroom-laundry pastels with a single loud tomato for "do it" moments.
Locked type: Jua (display, round and goofy) + IBM Plex Sans KR (body).
Section plan: hub = title block, boss card grid, footer note. Game = top bar, title, canvas stage, step checklist + actions, result card.
Asset plan: all art drawn as inline SVG / canvas (image generation unavailable on the account's plan). Share image = screenshot of the hub.
CTA inventory: 탈탈 털기 (wobble), 다 갰다! (chunky tomato press), 그냥 뭉치기 (dashed, sheepish), 새 시트 꺼내기 (underlined text), boss cards (tilt lift).
